**1. The "Joby" play: Urban Air Mobility (UAM) Chaos Management**

Investors love UAM because it’s the "Future of Transport," but the logistical nightmare is massive. Classical computers can handle 10 drones. They struggle with 1,000 air taxis in a storm.

The Feed: Real-time telemetry from 500+ aircraft (GPS, battery state, wind shear, vertiport occupancy).

The "Quantum Side" Algorithm: A Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) for dynamic de-confliction. When two flight paths intersect, it's easy. When 50 paths intersect due to a sudden microburst over Manhattan, it becomes a combinatorial explosion.

The Orchestrator’s Job: It recognizes "Normal Operations" and keeps them on the local edge (classical). The moment it detects a "High-Complexity Conflict Cluster," it shunts just that cluster to the quantum backend for a near-instant optimal re-route.

The Physical Demo: A 3D map (or physical model city) with moving LEDs representing taxis. You simulate a "weather event," and the Orchestrator dashboard shows a "Quantum Offload" light turning green as the taxis instantly reorganize without colliding.
